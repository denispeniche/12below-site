Questions

What faculty usually ask

Does it cost me anything?

No. We cover production, hosting and promotion

Who owns my content?

You do. We only receive a non-exclusive licence to publish it

How am I compensated?

Through the royalty pool and referral commission, at your founding rate

I work for a conference or university. Can I still teach?

Usually, yes. We'll help you request your employer's approval

How much of my time does it take?

A planning call and one recording day

In which languages can I teach?

Portuguese, Spanish or English, with French to follow

What topics are you looking for?

Bible, leadership, ministry, family, health, stewardship and more

Can I leave the program later?

Yes. The exit terms are in your agreement
