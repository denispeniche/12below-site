Script Notes

A working production script for a 38–42 minute documentary, built from the Version 1 concept. Each scene is a two-column table: what we see on the left, what we hear on the right.

PresidentOn camera, sync sound. Kent Island lines are from the concept. Priority setups are talking points, not lines to read.
NarratorVoice only, recorded in studio. Warm, pastoral, unhurried.
Exec. SecretaryOn camera, sit-down. Outline of what she covers; she speaks in her own words.
Study PresenterOn camera. Outline only until the study is final.
TestimonyFive subjects, on camera, unscripted. Interview guides are at the end.
  • Underlined words in [brackets] are placeholders: a stat to confirm, a name, or a quote to pull from an interview.
  • Timecodes follow the concept and will move in the edit.

Full Spoken Script

Every word heard in the film, in order, by speaker. The President's lines are written to sound like him speaking, so he can say them in his own words rather than read them. Testimonies stay unscripted.

Testimony Interview Guides

The testimonies are unscripted. These questions draw out a 45–60 second story. Ask for answers in full sentences that don't depend on the question.
