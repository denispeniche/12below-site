Frequently asked questions

What is Advent Institute?+
Advent Institute is the flagship learning hub for the world church — one place for courses, certifications, leadership development, mentorship, community, and events, available in English, Español, Português, and Français
What's included in a membership?+
Every plan includes full access to our pathway library, verified certificates, offline downloads, and your Learning Companion — team and organization plans add live mentorship circles and shared reporting
Can my whole church or conference join together?+
Yes — the Church & Ministry Team and Conference & Union plans are built for groups, with shared dashboards and group hierarchy that mirrors your organization's real structure
Are certifications recognized by conferences and unions?+
Certification pathways are developed with conference-level faculty and issue a shareable, verifiable credential — recognition toward formal tracks like ordination varies by conference; check with your local leadership
Is there a free trial?+
Yes — every new member can try a full lesson free, and every paid plan is backed by a 30-day money-back guarantee
